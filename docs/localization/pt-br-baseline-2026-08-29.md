## Interpretation

Brazilian geography and Portuguese browser-language traffic both show measurable, engaged demand. The volumes are modest, so this evidence supports the current eight-page, one-locale pilot—not a broader rollout or a paid translation-platform commitment.

Country and browser-language dimensions describe different populations and must not be added together. The leading Brazilian GSC queries were primarily English-language Midjourney searches, so localized search demand still needs to be proven after launch.

## Post-launch comparison

After an approved launch, compare localized pages with their English controls for at least 42 days. Expansion remains gated on at least 100 localized organic impressions, 5 localized organic clicks, 10 combined prompt copies, saves, and newsletter CTA clicks, plus one confirmed attributed newsletter subscription and no technical SEO regressions.
